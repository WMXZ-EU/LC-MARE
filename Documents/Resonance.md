
# Bubble resonance under pressure

## Boyle's equation
$$
P(z)V(z)=P_0V_0=const
$$

## Bubble volume
$$
V(z)=\frac{4}{3}\pi a^3
$$
$$
V(z)=V_0\frac{P_0}{P(z)}=\frac{V_0}{(1+0.1z)}
$$
$$
a(z)=\frac{a_0}{(1+0.1z)^{1/3}}
$$

## Minnaert resonance
$$
f=\frac{1}{2\pi a}\big(\frac{3\gamma P_A}{\rho}\big)^{1/2}
$$
$$
f(z)=\frac{1}{2\pi a}\big(\frac{3\gamma P_0}{\rho}\big)^{1/2}(1+0.1z)^{1/2}
$$
$P_0$ = 100 kPa
$\rho$ = 1000 $kg/m^3$
$\gamma$ = 1.4
$$
f(z)\times a=3.26 (1+0.1z)^{1/2} \ m/s
$$

## Clay and Medwin (from Cato)
$$
f=3.25\frac{(1+0.1z)^{1/2}}{a}
$$

## Test
$$
f=3.26\frac{(1+0.1z)^{1/2}}{a_0}(1+0.1z)^{1/3}
$$
$$
f=3.26\frac{(1+0.1z)^{5/6}}{a_0}
$$
